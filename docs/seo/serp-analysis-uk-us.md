Prepared 10 Oct 2026. Supersedes the worldwide edition in `serp-analysis.md` (9 Oct 2026), because the scope is now **the UK and the US**. Method: Ajcreationz SEO Operating Playbook v6.0 (§8 SERP research, §9 competitor gaps), Audit Playbook v2.0 (§4 live SERP protocol, §5 competitor snapshot) and Content Playbook v7.0 (§7 pre-writing workflow).

Evidence labels: **OBSERVED** (dated result seen in this analysis), **RESEARCH** (third-party claim, directional), **OPERATING STANDARD**, **HYPOTHESIS**, **LIMITED CONFIDENCE**.

### US / generic-intent queries (OBSERVED)

From the 9 Oct capture and today's additions: Orbit Media, WebFX, Thrive, Focus Digital and Omni Online Strategies lead agency results; Dribbble, Bookipi, Arounda and Abstrakt lead web-design cost queries; Fiverr and Contra freelancers dominate GoHighLevel and A2P 10DLC fix queries; vendor blogs (Softr, Builts, RSLA, Layer3) own "GoHighLevel vs HubSpot".

### What this tells us

1. **Cost and comparison queries are won by the people who sell the service** (OBSERVED, nearly every price result). That is a gap for a page that shows sourced ranges for both markets side by side.
2. **UK results contain UK-specific price guides in pounds; US results contain dollar guides.** Few pages show both. Our service pages now carry a USD table and a GBP table (HYPOTHESIS: a differentiator for businesses that sell in both markets).
3. **Directories and listicles sit above most agencies** on commercial queries (DesignRush, Sortlist, Clutch, GoodFirms, Semrush Agency Partners). Listing there is a fast route to visibility (OPERATING STANDARD).
4. **Trust badges lead:** Shopify Plus Partner, Google Partner, Meta Partner, review counts ("250+ five-star reviews"). We hold none that we can show, so we do not claim any.
5. **The GoHighLevel space is full of low-price freelancers** (setup "from $500") and vendor self-promotion. Compliance depth (A2P 10DLC, UK GDPR/PECR) is the credible way in.
6. **UK sources disagree with US ones on price**, and sources disagree with each other. Ranges on the site are labelled as market figures with named sources.

| Positioning | Creative, brand-led studio: brand identity, marketing strategy, creative design, content writing, AI automation | Web, search, ads and CRM: WordPress, Shopify, custom sites, SEO, Meta and Google Ads, video editing, GoHighLevel, plus brand identity |
| Audience signal | Not market-specific | UK and US businesses (body copy, pricing in GBP and USD, `areaServed`) |
| Content type | Portfolio and agency overview | Service guides with sourced pricing, method, FAQs; case studies with brief and deliverables |
| Shared | The four portfolio projects and the team | Rewritten text; each case study links to the main-site project page |

**Recommendations for the main site (not changed by this work):**
- Do not target UK or US head terms on `.co` for services this site covers (WordPress, Shopify, SEO, ads, GoHighLevel). Keep `.co` for the creative and brand angle.
- Brand identity overlaps; the main site should lean on creative direction and portfolio, this site on the commercial query "brand identity agency".
- Align facts: the main site states 500+ projects and an office in Faisalabad, Pakistan, while this site makes no project-count or location claims. Decide what is true before either site publishes numbers. Fix the main site's title ("Home - Ajcreationz Agency"), meta description, duplicate H1 and the "Novation" typo.
- Link the two sites (done here via `sameAs` and case-study links); add the reverse link from `.co`.

## 6. Gaps we cannot close yet

- **Proof:** four portfolio projects, five attributed testimonials copied from the main site (no company names), no verified review profiles, no partner badges. Do not claim badges you do not hold.

- **Authority:** a new domain with few links.
