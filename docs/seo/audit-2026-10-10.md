## 1. Executive summary

**Health score: about 70/100** (crawlability 75, on-page 65, architecture 70, equal weights; the other domains are excluded). This is the code and build state, not live-verified.

**What is already in good shape on `.co.uk`:**

- `metadataBase` and canonical tags use `ajcreationz.co.uk`.
- `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` exist (14 URLs).
- A www-to-apex redirect is in place.
- The site has JSON-LD (`ProfessionalService`, `WebSite`, plus `Service`, `Breadcrumb` and `FAQ` on service pages).
- It has 11 service pages (about 900 words each), About, Work, Contact, Privacy and Terms, each with unique titles and one H1.

**Where you're lagging, in priority order:**

1. **Two domains, one business, overlapping services.** `ajcreationz.co` (main, WordPress) and `ajcreationz.co.uk` (this site) describe the same agency. If the `.co.uk` is global, it competes with your own main site for the same queries and the same brand name, and Google may pick one and suppress the other. Decide the role of `.co.uk` (see §3, F1).
2. **Contradictory trust claims across the two sites.** Main site: "500+ projects, 500+ clients, 10+ awards", based in Faisalabad, Pakistan. This site: "40+ brands", "5.0 rating", "3+ years", "started as a two-person studio". Inconsistent facts weaken trust and entity understanding.
3. **The main site itself has basic SEO gaps.** Title "Home - Ajcreationz Agency", no meta description, H1 repeated twice, the typo "Novation", no structured data seen, and service items with no page links in what I could fetch.
4. **No brand visibility yet.** "AJ Creationz" returned no page for you, and look-alike names (AJ Creativez, AJ Digital Agency) outrank you.
5. **Thin proof.** The Work page is about 110 words, with no case-study pages. Testimonials are unlinked and have no review schema.
6. **No measurement visible** (no GA4/GTM tag in the HTML). Search Console verification is supported through an env var but I can't confirm it's set.
