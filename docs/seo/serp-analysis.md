## 2. What the global SERPs look like (OBSERVED, 9 Oct 2026)

Across all eight service queries, results fell into five groups:

1. **Agency service pages**, mostly US (Orbit Media, WebFX, Thrive, Focus Digital, Omni Online Strategies) plus UK/EU agencies that rank globally because they target no city (Reboot Online, Koozai).
2. **Offshore and white-label providers**, especially India (SunTec India, W3Era, ControlF5) selling on price. Several offer **white-label** work to other agencies.
3. **Freelance marketplaces and profiles**: Fiverr, Contra, Upwork-style listings, especially for GoHighLevel and video editing.
4. **"Best agencies" listicles and directories** (Dribbble, Digital Agency Network, Influencer Marketing Hub, Sortlist, Clutch-style), mostly written by agencies that list themselves.
5. **Cost guides** ("how much does X cost"), every one published by someone who sells the service.

**Implications**

- **RESEARCH:** Head terms without a location are crowded and led by established US agencies. A new domain should not expect to win "web design agency" soon; target the **specific platform, service and segment** terms first.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** The best early wins are narrower: cost guides with sourced ranges, platform-specific pages, and segment pages (for example "for startups", "for ecommerce brands").
- **Price pressure is real.** Offshore providers and marketplaces set low anchors (a GoHighLevel setup listed from $500; short-form edits from $25). Compete on strategy, tracking and integration across services, not on lowest price.
- **Trust signals dominate.** Competitors lead with partner badges (Google Partner, Shopify Partner, Meta Business Partner), client counts and reviews. See the gap list in Section 7.

## 6. International SEO: technical decisions (OPERATING STANDARD unless noted)

1. **Domain.** The site runs on `ajcreationz.co.uk`. A country-code domain like `.co.uk` signals the UK to Google and **cannot be geo-targeted elsewhere** (**RESEARCH**; confirm in Google Search Central, "Locale-specific URLs"). For a worldwide business, use a generic domain. `ajcreationz.co` is already in your Hostinger account, and Google has listed `.co` among extensions it treats as generic (verify before relying on it); a `.com` is the other common choice. Moving means 301-redirecting every old URL, updating canonicals and the sitemap, and re-verifying Search Console. Decide **before** the site accumulates links and rankings.
2. **One English version, no geo-targeting.** Do not set a country target in Search Console. Use `lang="en"`. Done in code this session (`locale: en`, `areaServed: Worldwide`, `inLanguage: en`).
3. **hreflang.** Not needed until you publish language or country variants. Plan folder structure now (`/es/`, `/ar/` and so on) if translation is likely.
4. **Currency and units.** Pricing tables use USD with a note that figures vary by region. Add a currency selector only if you publish your own prices.
5. **Speed worldwide.** Confirm the host or CDN serves quickly in your target regions. The Hostinger dashboard shows CDN enabled; run PageSpeed Insights and WebPageTest from several locations.
6. **Time zones in the contact flow.** Add a time-zone or preferred-hours field to the contact form and booking, since enquiries now arrive from anywhere.
7. **Structured data.** Organization and Service schema now declare `areaServed: Worldwide`. If you register a business address, add it.
8. **Legal pages.** The privacy and terms pages need a lawyer's review for the jurisdictions you serve (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and so on).

## 7. Gaps and differentiation

**Where we can credibly differ (HYPOTHESES to test)**
1. **Sourced, transparent pricing** across every service, labeled as market figures. Most competitors publish cost guides only to sell.
2. **One team across the funnel:** brand, site, video, ads, SEO and CRM, with the pages cross-linked into a single loop.
3. **Honest platform advice** (custom vs template; GoHighLevel vs HubSpot) where competitors push their own offer.
4. **Compliance detail** for messaging and tracking in several regions, including US A2P 10DLC, which many generalist agencies skip.
5. **Premium-over-offshore positioning:** strategy, tracking and integration, rather than lowest price.

**Gaps we cannot close yet**
- **Proof:** three portfolio items, no reviews, no verified partner badges. Do not claim badges you do not hold. Becoming a Shopify Partner, Google Partner and Meta Business Partner are achievable and would match what competitors lead with.
- **Authority:** a new domain with few links and no directory listings.
- **Market presence:** nothing yet shows where clients are, which blocks credible market pages.
